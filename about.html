Boise Born & Raised

Our Journey in the Treasure Valley

In 2009, Summit Ridge Roofing opened with a single truck and a solid commitment to quality. The roofing industry is often known for high-pressure sales and temporary fixes. We chose a different path, choosing transparent pricing, GAF-certified training, and a focus on long-term performance.

Over the last fifteen years, we have kept our operations local and family-focused. We do not use third-party sales networks. When you call, you speak to professionals who understand Idaho weather patterns, local building codes, and the structural integrity required to withstand heavy winter snow and intense summer heat.

Every roof replacement we complete is built to architectural standards, ensuring clean valley lines, proper attic ventilation, and strict manufacturer-warranty compliance.
